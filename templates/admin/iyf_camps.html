{% if current_user.role == 'admin' %} Manage Camps {% endif %}

Donations collected from students at IYF camps. These are recorded against the IYF Camps campaign and get a real receipt number like any other donation, so they appear in the Donations Log and the 10BD exports. They are not 80G-eligible, and nothing is emailed or WhatsApped to students — most of this data is entered well after the money was collected, so receipts are generated for your records and can be downloaded from the Donations Log.
