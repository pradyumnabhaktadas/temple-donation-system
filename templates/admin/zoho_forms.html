🔗 Zoho Forms

One row per Zoho Form that takes money. This is what lets receipts be issued automatically: Razorpay tells us a payment was captured and which form it came from, and this table says what that form's donations are for.

The donor's name comes from the form's own webhook call, matched on the transaction ID — so the one thing each form needs is a webhook configured to send that ID. A form with a campaign set then gets its donations receipted on its own. Without one, its payments are reported in the reconciliation summary instead — never filed under a guessed campaign, because that would quietly corrupt every figure this app reports on.
