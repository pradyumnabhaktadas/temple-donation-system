◌ What we do
🛕

Deity Worship & Temple Construction

Daily seva for Sri Sri Rukmini Dwarkadish, Sri Sri Gaur Nitai and Sri Sri Jagannath Baldeva Subhadra, and the ongoing construction of the Dwarka temple.
🪔

Festivals & Celebrations

Janmashtami, Radhashtami, Gaura Purnima, Rama Navami, Govardhan Puja, Diwali and more, celebrated with the community throughout the year.
📖

Youth Preaching

College preaching, camps and outreach programs that bring young people closer to Krishna consciousness and Vedic wisdom.
🍛

Food for Life

Annadan -- distributing sanctified prasadam so that no one in our community goes without a meal.
🏠

Community Support (BACE)

Upkeep and support for BACE properties that house and sustain the wider community of devotees.
🐄

Cow Protection & Sanatan Dharma

Go-seva and outreach dedicated to spreading Sanatan Dharma, in the tradition Srila Prabhupada carried across the world.
